Student consent

Please read before you submit. By taking part in the Academy you agree to the following.

  • You work on case studies (fictional patients) under your mentor, a registered dietitian. Real patients are visible to you only if your mentor shares them, for reading only, with contact details hidden.
  • Real patient information is confidential. You will not copy, photograph, screenshot, download, print or write down a real patient's details, and you will not discuss a real patient outside the Academy, including on social media.
  • You will not try to identify or contact a real patient, or open any record your mentor has not shared with you.
  • Your login is personal. You will not share it, and you will change your first password when you sign in. Tell your mentor at once if someone else may have used your login.
  • We keep your name, email, phone, institution, cohort, mentor and your activity in the Academy account, to create your login and supervise your learning. We do not sell your data. You can ask to see, correct or delete it, unless we must keep it by law.
  • This is training, not practice. You will not give nutrition or medical advice to real patients or present yourself as a clinician.
  • If you break these rules the Academy can remove your access. You can leave at any time by telling your mentor in writing. Your confidentiality duties continue after you leave.
