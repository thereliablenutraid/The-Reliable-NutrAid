How it works

What happens, step by step

Every patient is looked after in the same clear order, so nothing is skipped and your plan always rests on a clear diagnosis.

1

We look at the whole picture

Your doctor and dietitian ask about your health, medicines, daily routine, food and drink, and review any test results or body-composition reports you share.

2

We explain what we found

Your doctor makes the medical diagnosis. Your dietitian names the nutrition problems that matter most. Both explain it in plain words, so you know what the plan is for.

3

You get a plan that fits your life

Your doctor advises on treatment. Your dietitian gives goals, daily targets and meal guidance based on your condition and culture. Your plan is yours to use and to ask questions about.

4

We check progress together

At each follow-up your care team compares with where you started, celebrate what is working and change what is not.

What to bring to your first visit

  • Recent blood test or lab reports (PDF or photo)
  • Body-composition report, if you have one
  • A list of your medicines and supplements
  • A typical day of eating and drinking
  • Your questions and what you want to change

How a dietitian earns a specialty

Before seeing patients in a specialty, a dietitian goes through four stages. This is how we keep care consistent.

1. TrainingCompletes the clinic's course for that specialty.
2. SupervisedSees patients with a senior dietitian reviewing each case.
3. IndependentCares for patients in the specialty on their own.
4. ReviewerChecks the work of colleagues in training.
